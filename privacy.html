Train Tracker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-29

Train Tracker is a personal tool run by two people (Ethan Hauben and Jaclyn Hauben) for their own use. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

It records trains passing a camera I, Ethan Hauben, own, and sends the videos I choose to my own TikTok account's inbox so I can review and post them myself.

What it collects

The only TikTok data it handles is the login authorization for my own account (an access token) and the basic account details TikTok returns to confirm which account is connected. These are stored only in the secure keychain of my own computer. Videos, captions and train information are stored on my own computer and, optionally, in my own private cloud storage.

What it does not do

It does not collect data from anyone else. It does not sell, share or publish any data. It does not read my TikTok videos, followers, messages or other account content. It does not use TikTok data for advertising or analytics.

Video content

Videos show trains and public railway scenery. I try not to record identifiable people.

Deleting data

I can disconnect at any time with the tool's logout command and by removing the app in TikTok under Settings → Security & permissions → Apps. Doing so deletes the stored login. Stored videos can be deleted from my own computer or storage.
